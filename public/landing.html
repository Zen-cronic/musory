Music for bars, gyms and clubs

Tap forget me once and every venue's music stops using your taste.

Music changes how long guests stay. Musory plays what a venue's regulars liked, pooled in anonymous groups of 3 or more, and forgets any guest in one tap.

Open the live roomWatch the 1-minute video

The live room runs from the team's laptop during the event.

1

Check in and like

Scan the venue's QR code, choose what to share, then tap I like this.

2

The room makes a track for the crowd

When the crowd's likes shift, ElevenLabs Music makes a new track from their taste and the room crossfades into it.

3

Forget me

One tap deletes your likes, your taste and any music made from it. The room goes back to what it would play without you.
