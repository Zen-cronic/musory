Start here

  1. Open Copper Room and Basement 2 on the laptop. Press Start the room on one of them for sound.
  2. Scan Copper Room's QR code with your phone (or open the guest page here). Check in, then tap I like this a few times.
  3. Watch Next change on the room screen. It shows the pick with memory next to the pick with no memory.
  4. Tap Go to Basement 2 on your phone. Your taste applies there at once.
  5. Tap Forget me. The receipt shows the records about you going to 0, and both rooms re-pick.
  6. On the proof page, run the database refusals: 13, 121 and E11000.
